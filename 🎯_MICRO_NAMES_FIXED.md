## 🎯 **How It Works Now**

### Multi-Dimensional Naming

Each micro segment is named based on **TWO KEY CHARACTERISTICS**:

```
PRIMARY Characteristic × SECONDARY Characteristic = Unique Name

Examples:
- Digital × Portfolio = "Digital Multi-Product Users"
- Value × Digital = "Wealthy Digital Clients"
- Velocity × Digital = "Frequent Digital Transactors"
- Portfolio × Tenure = "Established Relationship Customers"
- Value × Velocity = "Premium Active Bankers"
```

### 49 Possible Combinations

7 characteristics × 7 characteristics = **49 unique names**

**Characteristics Used**:
1. 📱 Digital Engagement
2. 📦 Portfolio Depth (# of products)
3. ⚡ Transaction Velocity
4. 💰 Account Value (deposits)
5. 💵 Income Level
6. 💳 Credit Utilization
7. ⏳ Account Tenure
