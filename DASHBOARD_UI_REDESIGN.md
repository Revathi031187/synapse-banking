### 5. **Visual Hierarchy Improvements**

#### **Typography:**
- **Header** - 2xl font, bold, tracking-tight
- **Card Titles** - lg/xl font, bold, clear
- **Metrics** - 2xl-3xl font for primary numbers
- **Labels** - xs font, uppercase for section headers
- **Body Text** - sm font, gray-600 for supporting text

#### **Spacing:**
- **Page Padding** - 6 units (24px) horizontal, 8 units (32px) vertical
- **Card Gap** - 6 units (24px) between cards
- **Internal Padding** - 4 units (16px) standard, 8 units (32px) for panels
- **Section Dividers** - 1px borders in gray-200

#### **Colors:**
- **Background** - Gray-50 for page, White for cards
- **Text** - Gray-900 for primary, Gray-600 for secondary
- **Accents** - Color-coded per segment (blue/red/green/tan/navy)
- **Borders** - Gray-200 standard, color accents for emphasis
- **Success** - Green-600 for revenue/spend
- **Info** - Blue-600 for interactive elements
