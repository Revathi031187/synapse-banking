## 🎓 **Key Learnings**

### **Why K-Prototypes?**
- Handles mixed numeric + categorical data
- Better than K-Means (numeric only)
- Better than K-Modes (categorical only)
- Preserves behavioral meaning

### **Why Feature-Based Scoring?**
- Transparent & explainable
- Easy to tune
- Fast inference (<100ms)
- Business stakeholders understand it

### **Why 3 Personas?**
- Statistical significance (>400 per persona)
- Clear behavioral distinctions
- Manageable for business
- Based on real customer research
