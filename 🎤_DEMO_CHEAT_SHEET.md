## 🎯 **COMMON QUESTIONS & ANSWERS**

### **Q: "Why are transaction numbers so low (0.5 - 1.5)?"**
**A:** "These are major banking events - account openings, loan applications, credit activations - not daily purchases. A customer doesn't open a new account weekly! What matters is the relative difference: Traditional Members at 1.5 are 3x more active than Elite Savers at 0.47. That behavioral contrast drives our targeting."

### **Q: "How are segments created?"**
**A:** "K-Prototypes machine learning algorithm analyzes 50+ attributes - income, transaction patterns, digital behavior, product holdings, credit scores, tenure, and more. It discovers natural groupings we might miss manually. Updates automatically as customer behavior evolves."

### **Q: "What do you do with these segments?"**
**A:** "Three things:
1. **Personalize offers** - Right product to right customer at right time
2. **Optimize resources** - Focus relationship managers on high-value segments
3. **Prevent churn** - Different retention strategies for different personas"

### **Q: "How accurate is this?"**
**A:** "Silhouette score of 0.52 indicates moderate-to-good quality. We can drill down to 20 micro-segments for even more precision. And our recommendation engine shows product-fit scores so you see exactly how confident the AI is in each suggestion."
