## 📊 **New Metrics Added**

### **1. Revenue Metrics (💰 Business Critical)**

Every persona now includes:
- **Total Revenue (12 months)**: How much money this persona generates
- **Estimated Profit**: Revenue × profit margin (varies by persona type)
- **Average Customer Value**: Revenue per customer in the segment
- **Revenue Percentage**: Share of total company revenue
- **Profit Margin**: 35% for premium (Pro), 25% for mid-tier (Family), 15% for budget (Novice)

**Why it matters**: Executives care about revenue, not just customer counts. Now you can justify investments by persona profitability.

---

### **2. Confidence Scores (🎯 Quality Assurance)**

For each persona assignment, we now calculate:
- **Average Confidence**: How certain the model is (0-100%)
- **High/Medium/Low Confidence Counts**: Distribution of assignment certainty
- **Confidence Visualization**: Stacked bar charts showing confidence levels

**Why it matters**: Not all persona assignments are equally reliable. High confidence = actionable, low confidence = needs review.

---

### **3. Temporal/Seasonal Metrics (📅 Marketing Timing)**

New time-based insights:
- **Peak Season**: When this persona joins most (Spring/Summer/Fall/Winter)
- **Growth Rate**: 3-month customer acquisition trend
- **Seasonal Distribution**: Customer joins by season
- **New Customers (3 months)**: Recent acquisition velocity

**Why it matters**: Plan marketing campaigns when each persona is most active. Don't waste budget on off-season advertising.

## 🚀 **How to Use (For Non-Technical Users)**

### **Step 1: Run K-Prototypes**
1. Go to "Agent A: Personas" tab
2. Click green "Run K-Prototypes" button
3. Wait 5-10 seconds
4. See results!

### **Step 2: Read Executive Summary**
Look at the 4 boxes at top:
- **Total Revenue**: Good or bad compared to last quarter?
- **Profit**: Is margin healthy (>20% is good)?
- **Customers**: Growing or shrinking?
- **Accuracy**: Green checkmark = trust it. Yellow warning = be cautious.

### **Step 3: Identify Top Revenue Persona**
Look at "Revenue by Persona" chart:
- **Tallest green bar = most revenue**
- **Blue bar = profit**
- **Percentage cards below = revenue share**

**Action**: This persona gets top priority for retention programs!

### **Step 4: Plan Seasonal Campaigns**
Look at "Seasonal Acquisition" chart:
- **Tallest area = peak season for that persona**

**Action**: Schedule email campaigns 1 month before peak season.

### **Step 5: Export for Marketing**
1. Click "Export to CSV" button (top right)
2. Opens `academy_sports_personas.csv`
3. Upload to your email tool
4. Create segment-specific campaigns

**Example**:
- Filter for "Novice" persona
- Send "Camping 101: Beginner's Guide" email
- Filter for "Seasoned Pro" persona  
- Send "New MSR Tent Launch" email
