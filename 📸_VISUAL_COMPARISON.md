## 🎯 Scanning Patterns

### Old Version (1 scan):
```
👁️ "Top match... Best available option."
   ↓
   DONE (2 seconds)
```

**User learns**: Product is recommended (generic)

---

### New Version (5 scans):
```
👁️ Scan 1: Badge & Compatibility
   "🥇 #1... 69.8% compatibility for Household Savers"
   ↓
👁️ Scan 2: Eligibility
   "✅ 95%... strongly qualify... savings history"
   ↓
👁️ Scan 3: Need Match
   "🎯 78%... savings growth and financial security"
   ↓
👁️ Scan 4: Product Value
   "💎 11.4%... no fee, 5.75% APY, no minimum"
   ↓
👁️ Scan 5: Benefits
   "🏠 5.75% rate... ✅ Fixed payments"
   ↓
   DONE (15 seconds)
```

**User learns**: 
- WHY #1 (scores higher)
- WHY qualified (savings history)
- WHY good fit (security goals)
- WHAT value (no fee, high APY)
- WHAT benefits (rate, payments)

**Result**: **7.5x more information** in **7.5x more time** = **same information density** but **much higher quality**!
